About

I started as a frontend developer and designer, and over time moved into leading the people, processes, and practices that turn good ideas into well-built software.

My background spans e-commerce, fintech, SaaS, and AI. I've built component libraries from scratch, redesigned investment journeys for 2,000+ investors, grown engineering and design teams from 2 to lots, and helped grow a fund from zero to £77M. Good engineering and good user experience should reinforce each other when the team is set up right.

Right now I'm VP of Engineering at Memorify, a founding team member building an AI-powered memory preservation product from the ground up. I lead the engineering team, own delivery and technical standards, and work closely with the CEO and CXO to shape the product roadmap.

I care about shipping things properly, developing people, and keeping the codebase in a state that new engineers can actually get into quickly. I'm also fluent enough in design to speak both languages, which tends to save a lot of time.
