UI & UX Lead, June 2023 – July 2025

I built and led the design and frontend team at Risilience – one designer and two developers – and turned dense climate risk data into dashboards enterprise clients could actually use. Customer satisfaction rose 46% and self-service rose 22%, off the back of a component library and style guide that brought every product up to WCAG 2.2 compliance.

Problem

  • Dashboards were difficult to navigate, and dense climate risk data wasn't landing with users.
  • No design system or scalable UI components existed, so every screen was built from scratch.
  • Accessibility compliance was lacking across the platform.

What I did

  • Built and led the design and frontend team – one designer, two developers – from scratch.
  • Ran client interviews, stakeholder workshops, personas, and journey mapping to ground the redesign in real usage.
  • Developed a reusable component library and style guide, built in React and TypeScript, that brought every product up to WCAG 2.2 compliance.
  • Redesigned the dashboards with interactive charts and visualisations that turned complex datasets into actionable insight.

Impact

  • 46% increase in customer satisfaction.
  • 22% increase in customer self-service.
  • WCAG 2.2 compliance achieved across all products.
  • Standardised component library cut design-to-dev handoff time and improved consistency platform-wide.
