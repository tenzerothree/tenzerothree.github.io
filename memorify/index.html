VP of Engineering, November 2025 – present

I joined Memorify as a founding team member in November 2025 to build an AI-powered memory preservation product from the ground up. I started as Senior Product Design & Development Lead and moved into VP of Engineering as the company grew, taking on the engineering team, delivery, and technical standards while the founding engineer focuses on ML/AI.

{% include back-arrow.svg %}Back

Problem

  • No product, team, or codebase existed yet – everything had to be built from zero as a founding hire.
  • AI-driven memory organisation needed to be translated into something non-technical users could trust and understand.
  • Privacy and security had to be designed in from day one, not bolted on later.

What I did

  • Built end-to-end product systems combining AI-driven content recognition with intuitive UX.
  • Established a scalable design system and component architecture across the product.
  • Worked closely with the ML/AI team to translate complex memory organisation algorithms into user-facing features.
  • Moved from Senior Product Design & Development Lead into VP of Engineering, taking on the team, delivery, and technical standards, and working with the CEO and CXO to shape the roadmap.
  • Built the product cross-platform on React, TypeScript, and Node.js, with CI/CD on AWS.

Impact

  • Took the product from zero to a working cross-platform system as a founding engineer.
  • Grew into the engineering leadership role as the founding team scaled, owning delivery and technical direction.
  • Privacy and security built into the core of the product from the first release.
