Head of Tech, June 2019 – June 2023

At SyndicateRoom I led design and development together, rebuilding the investment journeys and dashboards used by 2,000+ investors. I grew the team from 2 to 6, led the migration to microservices, and built a modular component library that cut delivery cycles by 30% – contributing to a fund that grew from zero to £77M with 76% investor retention.

Problem

  • Investment journeys were inconsistent and hard to navigate.
  • No structured design system, and a legacy monolithic codebase slowed every change.
  • The team needed to scale fast to keep pace with fund growth.

What I did

  • Grew the design and development team from 2 to 6, mentoring juniors through to senior and lead roles.
  • Led the migration from a legacy monolith to microservices.
  • Rebuilt the investment flows and dashboards for 2,000+ investors, designing directly in Vue.js and React.
  • Built a modular component library that standardised the UI and cut delivery cycles by 30%.

Impact

  • Fund grew from zero to £77M deployed, with 76% investor retention.
  • 30% reduction in delivery cycles from the component library.
  • Team grew from 2 to 6, with a mentoring structure that took juniors to senior and lead level.
